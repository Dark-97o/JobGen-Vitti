# UI Rule: No Pills Above Headlines

## Critical Directives
1. **Never place pill badges, tag pills, or chip elements above headlines.**
2. When creating or editing sections, do NOT add eyebrow badges with text (e.g. `<div className="badge-pill">...</div>` or `<div className="who-pill-badge">...</div>`) above section headlines (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`).
3. Keep headline hierarchy clean, direct, and prominent without decorative pill labels above them.
